## Project Description
SafeRoute Survivor Shield AI is built on survivor dignity, ethical AI, institutional accountability, and actionable pathways — ensuring awareness campaigns are safe, respectful, and effective. It combines survivor testimony, generative AI campaigns, historical photos, and global risk map to expose exploitation, teach real signs, protect dignity, and mobilize communities into action.

## Antigravity chatbot to combat child and human trafficking
I am excited to share that I have successfully developed a web-based chatbot designed to combat human trafficking. This project integrates advanced assistant technology with an antigravity-inspired interface to create a responsive, user-friendly platform. The chatbot’s primary goal is to raise awareness, provide accessible information, and support individuals by connecting them to resources that help prevent and fight human trafficking. By combining innovation with social responsibility, this tool demonstrates how technology can be leveraged to address one of the most pressing global challenges.
