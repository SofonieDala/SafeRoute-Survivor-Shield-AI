# 🛡️ SafeRoute Survivor Shield AI

### AI-Powered Human Trafficking Awareness, Survivor Advocacy & Community Protection

SafeRoute Survivor Shield AI is an AI-powered platform designed to combat human trafficking through survivor-centered education, ethical AI, and public awareness. The project combines survivor testimony, generative AI, interactive storytelling, multilingual support, and a global risk map to help individuals recognize trafficking risks, protect vulnerable communities, and encourage informed action.

# 🚨 Problem

Human trafficking remains one of the world's most hidden crimes. Millions of victims go unidentified because communities often lack the knowledge to recognize warning signs or access reliable information.

Survivors also face significant barriers when sharing their experiences, while awareness campaigns frequently fail to provide engaging, accessible, and multilingual educational resources.

There is a need for a technology-driven platform that transforms survivor testimony into ethical, actionable education capable of reaching communities worldwide.

---

# 💡 Solution

SafeRoute Survivor Shield AI combines artificial intelligence with survivor advocacy to deliver an accessible public awareness platform.

The platform provides:

* 🗺️ Global trafficking risk map
* 📚 Interactive archive of documented trafficking cases
* 🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Survivor-centered storytelling
* 🤖 AI-powered awareness chatbot
* 📱 AI social media campaign generator
* 🌍 Multilingual content translation
* 📖 Educational resources on trafficking warning signs
* 🛡️ Community protection guidance
* 📊 Case analysis and institutional accountability resources
